Why we built this

Mission, Vision & Values

🎯
Mission
Empower Every Farmer
To provide Zambian smallholder farmers with intelligent, accessible, and locally relevant tools that transform climate and agronomic data into practical, profitable farming decisions regardless of literacy level, location, or resources.
🌍
Vision
A Food-Secure Zambia
To become the leading digital agricultural advisory platform in Zambia, connecting farmers, extension officers, and market data into a single ecosystem that drives food security, increases household income, and builds climate resilience across all ten provinces.
⭐
Core Values
What Guides Us
  • Farmer-first design: every feature solves a real farming problem
  • Scientific integrity: all recommendations backed by ZARI/FAO data
  • Accessibility: works on low-end devices, in 10 Zambian languages
  • Transparency: decisions are explained, not black-boxed
  • Local relevance: built specifically for Zambian crops and climate zones
Key Features

What ZamFarm Climate Connect Does

Eight integrated modules covering the full farming decision cycle from pre-season planning to market sale, guided by Machine Learning and real climate intelligence.

🤖
Yield Prediction
RandomForest ML model trained on 1,600 agronomic samples predicts total harvest in metric tonnes, factoring in crop type, hectarage, soil type, rainfall, temperature, and fertilizer use.
Machine Learning
🌱
Crop Recommendation
A second RandomForest classifier analyses current rainfall, temperature, and soil conditions against optimal growing parameters for all 8 crops to recommend the best match with confidence scores.
Classification
☁️
Climate Data Engine
Integrates with OpenWeatherMap API for real-time district weather. Ingests historical climate records, generates seasonal forecasts, and feeds live rainfall/temperature data into all predictions.
Climate Integration
💰
Investment Simulator
Allows farmers to model different investment scenarios, comparing expected ROI across all 8 crops before committing resources. Uses live market prices from ZNFU to ensure financial accuracy.
Financial Planning
🏆
Gamification Engine
5-level progression system, 13 achievement badges, points, streaks, and a district leaderboard — designed to reward consistent use and create healthy competition among farmers in the same zone.
Engagement
🛒
Farmer Marketplace
Peer-to-peer produce marketplace where farmers can list crops for sale with multi-photo galleries, GPS location, status tracking, and in-system buyer messaging — eliminating middlemen.
Market Access
👨‍🌾
Extension Officer Portal
Dedicated dashboard for agricultural extension officers to monitor all farmers in their zone, view prediction histories, send advisories, and track farmer engagement and progress scores.
Officer Tools
⚙️
Admin Control Panel
Full system administration — manage users, configure crop parameters and market prices, adjust gamification levels and badges, view analytics, and manage extension officer assignments.
Administration
👨‍🏭
Farm Assistant
AI-powered chatbot providing instant answers to farmer queries, helping with crop management, system functionality guidance, and offering personalized advice based on real-time data.
Support

Evidence-Based

Data Sources & References

All agronomic parameters, crop benchmarks, and climate thresholds are grounded in peer-reviewed and government-published Zambian data.

ZARI
Zambia Agriculture Research Institute
Ministry of Agriculture, Zambia
Crop variety trials, optimal growing parameters, base yield benchmarks, and soil management recommendations for all 8 supported crops.
zari.gov.zm
ZMD
Zambia Meteorological Department
Ministry of Transport, Zambia
Historical rainfall data, seasonal forecasts, and climate zone classifications used in the climate data engine and district-level weather fallbacks.
zmd.gov.zm
FAO
Food and Agriculture Organization
United Nations, Africa Regional Office
Sub-Saharan Africa crop production statistics, climate-smart agriculture guidelines, and drought-tolerant variety recommendations for smallholder farmers.
fao.org/africa
ZNFU
Zambia National Farmers Union
ZNFU Market Information Service
Weekly commodity price data for maize, soya, groundnuts, and other crops. The live market price fetch feature scrapes ZNFU's published price tables every 24 hours.
znfu.org.zm
FRA
Food Reserve Agency
Statutory Body, Government of Zambia
Strategic grain reserve management and government floor prices for maize and other staple crops — used as reference prices in the financial calculation engine.
frazambia.com
CIMMYT
Int'l Maize & Wheat Improvement Center
CGIAR Research Program on Maize
Improved seed variety performance data and drought-tolerant maize trial results specific to Southern African growing conditions — underpins the yield prediction model training data.
cimmyt.org
