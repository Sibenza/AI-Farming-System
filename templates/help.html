Quick Answers

You earn 10 points each day you log in and 50 points for every simulation you run. Points add up and move you through 5 levels — from Climate Novice 🌱 to Climate Master 👑 — unlocking badges along the way.
When the AI model is unavailable, the system uses a reliable agronomic calculation instead, and tells you honestly which one was used. Both give useful guidance — the AI badge 🤖 means the machine-learning model produced the figure.
Open any conversation, tap the blue microphone 🎤, allow microphone access, speak, then tap send. The other farmer can play it right inside the chat. Voice notes can be up to 2 minutes long.
Tap your profile photo at the top of the dashboard, then tap Edit Profile. You can change your photo, name, phone, district, village, and farm size.
Use Crop Recommendation for advice based on your exact conditions, and check the Season Forecast page's Rainfall Outlook — it estimates the coming season's rainfall and ranks the best crops for it.
